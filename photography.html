From The Vault: The Portraits
(2/8/24)

I was recently going through photos I had taken, and was surprised by how many photos I had chosen not to put in any of my series. So, I have decided to share some of my favorite portraits!

Cold
(12/12/23)

This series of photographs comes from my final project, "Tableau," for Intermediate Photography. I decided to call this collection "Cold" because every one of these images represents the feeling of being chilled in some way. My class loved these photographs, and I'm very proud of them!

Hour of Light
(12/1/23)

While home for Thanksgiving, I took these photos as the sun went down. The quality of the light was stunning, and I think these photos look great!

Self-Portraits
(11/12/23)

I recently took some self-portraits in my free time, and I'm so happy with how they turned out! I usually don't like photos of me, but I think these look good!
